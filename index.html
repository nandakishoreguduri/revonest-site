Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before the first campaign.

Can Revonest market a project before TG RERA registration?

Revonest will not publish a sales promotion, collect buyer interest or represent a project as available before the relevant registration and advertising information are verified.

Does Revonest guarantee leads, rankings, site visits or sales?

No. Revonest defines measurable stages and improves the journey using observed evidence.

Can you work with our current sales team or CRM?

The audit first documents your workflow, access, consent and reporting before any integration is recommended.

Do we have to replace our existing website?

Not necessarily. Revonest can first assess whether the current project page can be improved.
